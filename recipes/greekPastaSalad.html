Greek Pasta Salad

Description

This recipe is a delicious and refreshing salad that combines pasta with a variety of fresh ingredients. This is great for summer time, a quick snack, or as a side to a meal.

Note: This recipe makes 4 servings and takes about 15 minutes to prep and 10 minutes to cook (Roughly 2 1/2 hours altogether to include the set-aside time).

Ingredients

  • Olive Oil: 1/2 Cup
  • Red Wine Vinegar: 1/2 Cup
  • Garlic Powder: 1 1/2 Teaspoons
  • Dried Basil: 1 1/2 Teaspoons
  • Dried Oregano: 1 1/2 Teaspoons
  • Ground Black Pepper: 3/4 Teaspoon
  • White Sugar: 3/4 Teaspoon
  • Elbow Macaroni (Cooked): 2 1/2 Cups
  • Fresh Mushrooms (Sliced): 3 Cups
  • Cherry Tomatoes (Halved): 15
  • Red Bell Peppers (Sliced): 1 Cup
  • Crumbled Feta Cheese: 3/4 Cup
  • Green Onions (Chopped): 1/2 Cup
  • Whole Black Olives: 1 Can (4 Ounces)
  • (Optional) Pepperoni Sausage (Sliced) 3/4 Cup

Steps

  1. Step 1: In a large bowl, whisk together olive oil, vinegar, garlic powder, basil, oregano, black pepper, and sugar.
  2. Step 2: Add to bowl the cooked pasta, mushrooms, tomatoes, red peppers, feta cheese, green onions, olives, and pepperoni sausage. Toss to combine.
  3. Step 3: Cover bowl and place in refrigerator to let chill over night (or for at least 2 hours).
