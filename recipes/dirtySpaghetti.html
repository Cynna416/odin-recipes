One-Pan Dirty Spaghetti

Description

This spaghetti is made all in one pan which means: less clean-up and a meal with tons of flavor than if it were cooked the traditional way. Cooking it this way ensures that the pasta absorbs the flavors of everything, rather than the absence of flavor it would have if boiled separately. The "dirty" part of the name comes from it's similarity to "dirty rice", only in spaghetti form.

Note: This recipe makes 4 servings and takes about 20 minutes to prep and to cook (40 minutes altogether).

Ingredients

  • Olive Oil: 2 Tablespoons
  • Ground Beef: 8 Ounces
  • Yellow Onion (Diced): 1 Medium
  • Bell Pepper (Diced): 1 1/2 Cups
  • Celery (Diced): 1/2 Cup
  • Andouille Sausage (Diced Small): 4 Ounces
  • Onion Powder: 1/2 Teaspoon
  • Garlic Powder: 1/2 Teaspoon
  • Paprika: 1 Teaspoon
  • Ground Cumin: 1 Teaspoon
  • Dried Oregano: 1/4 Teaspoon
  • Dried Thyme: 1/4 Teaspoon
  • Kosher Salt: 1 1/2 Teaspoons
  • Cayenne Pepper: 1/4 Teaspoon
  • Freshly Ground Black Pepper: 1/2 Teaspoon
  • Beef Broth: 2 Cups
  • Spaghetti: 8 Ounces
  • Worcestershire Sauce: 1 Teaspoon
  • (Optional) Sliced green Onions: 1/2 Cup

Steps

  1. Add oil to a deep skillet on medium-high heat. Add ground beef and cook, stirring often, for 3-4 minutes (or until browned), breaking the meat up in the process.
  2. Add onion, bell peppers, celery, and andouille sausage. Cook and stir for 2 minutes. Season with onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, cumin, oregano, thyme, cayenne, black pepper, and salt. Cook and stir for 2 more minutes. Add broth and bring to a simmer.
  3. Break spaghetti in half and add to pan. Make sure spaghetti is distributed as evenly as possible and saturated. Cook on medium-high for 2 minutes. Uncover and stir, repeating this process every 2 minutes until pasta is done.
  4. Turn off heat. Add Worcestershire Sauce and stir. Serve with sliced green onions as garnish.
