5-Ingredient Salmon Teriyaki Bowl

Description:

This is a quick and easy recipe for a healthy and delicious bowl of Salmon Teriyaki over rice. It contains only 5 ingredients and comes out with epic flavor.

Note: This recipe makes 4 servings and takes about 15 minutes to prep and to cook (30 minutes altogether).

Ingredients:

  • Salmon Filets: 4-6 Ounces
  • Kosher Salt: 1/2 Teaspoon
  • Freshly Ground Black Pepper: 1/2 Teaspoon
  • Teriyaki Sauce: 1/2 Cup
  • Jasmine Rice (Cooked): 3 Cups
  • (Optional) Sriracha, or another Hot Sauce
  • Edamame (Shelled and prepared according to package directions): 2 Cups
  • Avocado (Peeled and Diced): 1

Steps:

  1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 450°F (235°C). Use foil to line a rimmed baking sheet and lightly grease.
  2. Step 2: Season salmon with salt and pepper. Spread 1 Tablespoon of teriyaki sauce on top of each filet. Place on the prepared baking sheet.
  3. Step 3: Place in oven and bake for 10-12 minutes, or until salmon flakes easily.
  4. Step 4: Prepare edamame according to package directions, if not already prepared and keep warm.
  5. Step 5: Divide rice between bowls and add edamame on top. Top with salmon and avocado.
  6. Step 6: Add remaining teriyaki sauce and sriracha (if using).
